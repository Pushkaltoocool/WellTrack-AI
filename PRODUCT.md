## Platform

adaptive

## Product Purpose

A personal student wellness companion combining daily check-ins, foreground motion recognition, wellness suggestions, goals, and stored trends. A wellness tool, not a medical device.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Expo SDK 58, React Native, TypeScript, Expo Router, and local SQLite. Preserve real tracking, recorded replay, supported step history, check-in validation, editable history, goals, model explanations, export, and deletion. Recorded replay stays separate from personal statistics. Data stays on the device; no account or cloud.

## Brand Commitments

The user requested the supplied `WellTrack AI — redesign.html` screens. Implement their Evening visual system and owl. Sample names, dates, and measurements are examples; display real stored data.

## Evidence on Hand

The implementation and README establish behavior. The supplied HTML covers Today, check-in, Movement, breathing, break timer, Journey, Settings, and owl variants.
