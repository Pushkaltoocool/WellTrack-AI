## Description

WellTrack AI is a quiet companion for busy student life. Take a minute each evening to note how you slept, studied, moved and felt, and WellTrack helps you see what makes your days better.

**Check in, in a minute**
Six gentle steps: sleep, study, screen time, movement, people, and how you're feeling. Edit any day.

**See your patterns**
Your week in review, plus patterns from your own data, like how your mood tends to change on days you sleep more. After a month, a small personal model learns which of your habits go with higher or lower stress. It runs entirely on your phone and only shows results when they beat a simple average.

**Movement that understands you**
Keep the app open while you walk, take the stairs or sit at your desk, and on-device activity recognition shows how your day is split. On iPhone, see your all-day steps.

**Small, doable suggestions**
Clear suggestions based on your entries, each with the reason it appeared. Turn one into a goal and build a streak.

**Exam season, handled**
Add exams and deadlines for countdowns, a reminder the evening before, and gentler suggestions in busy weeks.

**Wren, your AI coach (optional)**
Ask for a plan for tonight, help before an exam, or a reflection on your week. Wren only sees a summary you agree to share, and it's not a therapist or a substitute for professional care.

**Calm tools**
Focus timer, box breathing and a page of support options for harder days.

**Private by design**
Your journal lives on your phone. An account is optional and only backs up your data for you. No ads, no tracking. Lock the app with Face ID or fingerprint. Export or delete everything at any time.

WellTrack AI is a wellness tool, not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition. If you feel unsafe, contact local emergency services.
